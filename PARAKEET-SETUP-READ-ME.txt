KICK CLIPPER - PARAKEET SETUP

Parakeet and Moment AI are TWO separate stages:

1) TRANSCRIPTION (audio -> text)
   Provider: Together NVIDIA Parakeet
   Required: TOGETHER_API_KEY

2) MOMENT ANALYSIS (transcript -> best clips)
   Provider: Grok/xAI OR OpenAI
   Required: LLM_API_KEY saved in Admin > Moment analysis AI

Recommended .env settings:

TRANSCRIPTION_PROVIDER=parakeet
TOGETHER_API_KEY=YOUR_TOGETHER_KEY
PARAKEET_MODEL=nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
PARAKEET_LANGUAGE=en
PARAKEET_CONCURRENCY=4
PARAKEET_CHUNK_SECONDS=1800
PARAKEET_CHUNK_OVERLAP_SECONDS=8
PARAKEET_VERIFY_CREDENTIALS=true
PARAKEET_LOCAL_FALLBACK=false
PARAKEET_TIMEOUT_SECONDS=180
PARAKEET_MAX_RETRIES=4
ASR_MAX_CONCURRENCY=4

Before a long VOD:
  python verify_parakeet.py

It must report PASS. During a VOD the terminal should show:
  [transcribe] provider=parakeet
  [parakeet] VERIFIED ...
  [parakeet] OK ...

If a Moment AI key is missing, the dashboard now says exactly that instead of implying Parakeet is missing.
