How it works

Point it → We watch the whole thing → Clips land in your library.

No manual downloading, no hunting for a stream URL, no guessing whether a job actually finished. Every job is a tracked pipeline with a real, honest status.

01

Point it at a Kick channel or VOD

Paste kick.com/username or a VOD link — that's it. We resolve the real playback source server-side; you never touch a CloudFront manifest URL.

02

Search the transcript, inspect the shortlist

For supported stream sources, audio is pulled first and transcribed locally. Overlapping transcript windows locate candidates. A bounded context review checks the strongest moments before final ranking; optional video review inspects those sections. Silent events outside the shortlist can still be missed.

03

Clips land in your library

Captioned, face-cropped, scored, with a suggested title and a "why this clip" reason — ready to download. Nothing is marked done until it's actually sitting in your library.

The actual pipeline

Every job is a tracked state machine, not a black box

This is the real stage list a job moves through — the same one the dashboard shows you live.

queuedWaiting for a worker slot.
resolving_sourceTurning your URL into a real playback source, server-side.
audio_pullPulling audio only — video comes later, only for kept clips.
transcribingLocal speech-to-text, word-level timestamps.
scoringEvery window judged by the LLM against your threshold.
renderingCrop, face-track, burn in captions, encode — only for clips that cleared the bar.
indexing_libraryClips are counted into your library from the database, not a log line.
settling_creditsHold is captured or refunded based on what actually happened.
Every possible outcome, handled honestly

A job never lies about what it did

completed

At least one clip cleared your threshold. Credits for the source minutes are captured; clips are in your library.

completed_empty

The full stream was scanned, nothing cleared the bar. Credits are returned automatically — this is the "empty VOD never costs you" promise.

failed

Something on our end broke (source resolve, LLM, encoder). Credits are returned in full and the reason is shown to you.

partial

Some clips finished before a later failure. Those clips stay in your library; credits are settled for the work actually done.
