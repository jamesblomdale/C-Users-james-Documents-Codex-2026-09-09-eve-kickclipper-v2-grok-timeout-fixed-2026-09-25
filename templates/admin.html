Pipeline quality & visual context

Before final ranking, a second editorial review checks the strongest candidates against surrounding dialogue. Optional frame review sends up to six images per reviewed candidate to your configured AI provider. It cannot see events between frames or discover silent moments. Leave the model blank for transcript-only processing.

Gemini requires GEMINI_API_KEY in your server .env. It uploads only reviewed candidate videos, requests agentic inspection and attempts to delete each upload afterward. It does not scan the entire VOD for silent events.

At most one additional text request and one optional visual request per reviewed candidate. Set 0 to disable the second pass. Request limits are not a dollar budget.

GPU: try batch 8 with Turbo. CPU: begin at batch 1. Larger batches use more memory. Beam 1 is the fast default; higher beams may improve difficult speech but take longer.
